MINISTERIUM OF WIZARDS

OFFICIAL DEGREE // CLASSIFIED

The Germanic Exile

INQUISITORIAL NOTE: Archival audits show a temporary, highly anomalous deviation from the subject's baseline behavior. For an unexplained duration, the subject abruptly halted all programmatic and computational output to focus entirely on acquiring foreign linguistics-specifically, mastering the German tongue.

Rather than returning immediately to his digital domains upon completion, the subject pivoted into an unexpected role, remaining stationed at the facility to instruct incoming cohorts. Intercepted local reports suggest the environment fostered unusual cooperative behavior, with the subject spending considerable time studying alongside and teaching civilian assets.

While inquisitors initially flagged this prolonged detour as an unproductive social diversion, surveillance logs confirm the period was marked by high operational morale and low systemic friction. The subject has since returned to his terminal workspace, though the linguistic subroutines appear to have permanently integrated into his cognitive framework.

Status: Filed under anomalies. Subject has resumed primary coding operations.

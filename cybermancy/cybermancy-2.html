TO THE FELLOWSHIP

HTML & CSS

Technically speaking, HTML was the very first language I ever encountered, back when I was quite small. At the time, I didn't see the point of it at all. Without knowing anything about CSS or JavaScript, raw markup just looked ugly and formatting felt like an absolute chore. For a long time, I honestly wished I could erase that entire memory.

It wasn't until much later that I realized how important that language actually was. Deciding to give it another try, I dived into "The Odin Project"- which is an exceptionally well-crafted grimoire for learning web architecture.

The course warns that most practitioners breeze through HTML and CSS just to rush ahead to JavaScript. But my path took a different turn. I never actually made it to JavaScript; the ministerium started monitoring my node a bit too strictly, forcing me to exercise caution since a completely free, open curriculum like that operates entirely outside official oversight. Coupled with the sudden influx of heavy academic coursework, I had to pause the JavaScript module, though I will likely revisit it in the future.

Despite stopping short, mastering the core of HTML and CSS allowed me to forge one of my favorite project yet: a dedicated portal where I can release my writings directly to the fellowship across the web, making these dispatches infinitely more accessible.
